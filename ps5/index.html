PS5 / PS5 Pro

Scene Launch

Aim at anything in Leonida, press square, and the frame becomes the token image.

Five steps

  1. Aim

    Open the overlay and put the reticle on anything.

  2. Capture

    The frame under the reticle is captured and tagged with its place.

  3. Name

    A name and ticker are suggested from the scene. Edit them or keep them.

  4. Confirm

    Check the mint fee, wallet and supply. Circle cancels.

  5. Launch

    The token is minted on Solana and listed in the in-game markets.

Limits

  • One launch per 24h

    Per linked wallet.

  • Mint fee in SOL

    Paid from the linked wallet. Shown before you confirm.

  • Captures kept 30 days

    Then removed, unless launched.

  • Wallet link by code

    Phantom, Solflare, Backpack or Glow.
